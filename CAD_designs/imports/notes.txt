In this directory can you place yout STL files for importing them into your designs

The used STL models can be retrieved from the importer of yout hubmotor, i use Quinder 48v hubmotors
The used STL models for the casterwheels are from a wheelbarrow wheel and can be downloaded from the next link https://grabcad.com/library/wheelbarrow-wheel-4-80-4-00-8-1
